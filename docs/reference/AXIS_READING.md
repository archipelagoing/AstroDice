# Natal Chart Axis Reading — Reference

This document preserves the original chart-analysis framework. For the running app, see the [README](../../README.md); for current visual identity, see [THEMING.md](../THEMING.md).

## Purpose

Analyze the attached circular natal chart as **six opposing house axes**
rather than as twelve isolated houses.

The six axes are:

1.  1st House ↔ 7th House --- Self ↔ Partnership
2.  2nd House ↔ 8th House --- Personal ↔ Shared
3.  3rd House ↔ 9th House --- Information ↔ Meaning
4.  4th House ↔ 10th House --- Roots ↔ Public Life
5.  5th House ↔ 11th House --- Creation ↔ Community
6.  6th House ↔ 12th House --- Daily Systems ↔ Inner World

Use the attached birth chart as the source of truth.

# STEP 2 --- DETERMINE THE ACTUAL CONTENTS OF EACH HOUSE

IMPORTANT:

Do NOT treat a house as if it contains only the sign on its cusp.

For every house:

1.  Identify the exact cusp degree.
2.  Identify the next house cusp.
3.  Determine every zodiac sign the house passes through between those
    points.
4.  List all planets/points physically located inside that house.
5.  Identify any intercepted signs.
6.  Identify any signs duplicated on consecutive house cusps.

Example:

A house beginning at 28° Libra and ending at 2° Sagittarius contains:

-   28°--30° Libra
-   all 30° of Scorpio
-   0°--2° Sagittarius

Therefore Scorpio is fully contained within that house.

Do this geometrically from the chart rather than assuming "House X =
Sign Y."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# STEP 3 --- IDENTIFY INTERCEPTIONS AND DUPLICATIONS

Explicitly determine:

### Intercepted signs

A sign is intercepted when the entire 30° sign lies inside a house
without appearing on a house cusp.

For each intercepted sign, state:

-   sign
-   house
-   opposite intercepted sign
-   opposite house
-   planets located in either intercepted sign

### Duplicated cusp signs

Identify signs appearing on two consecutive house cusps.

Explain how these structural features affect the six-axis reading.

## Planetary Story

Explain how planets on either side of the axis interact conceptually.

Consider:

-   Which side contains more planets?
-   Is one side empty?
-   Are planets located in opposing signs?
-   Are any planets dignified, exalted, debilitated, etc.?
-   Are the Sun or Moon involved?
-   Are the Nodes involved?
-   Is an angle involved (ASC/DSC or IC/MC)?
-   Is there a stellium?
-   Is an intercepted planet involved?

Do not claim that two planets form an astrological aspect unless their
actual degrees support that aspect.

## Central Axis

Identify the axis with the greatest **structural emphasis** based on
observable chart features such as:

-   number of planets
-   luminaries
-   angularity
-   stelliums
-   nodes
-   interceptions

Explain the evidence.

Do not simply choose whichever interpretation sounds most dramatic.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Overall Architecture

Give a concise synthesis of how the six axes work together.

Focus on recurring tensions, repeated sign polarities, planetary
concentrations, and movement between neighboring houses.

The goal is to describe the chart as an interconnected system rather
than a collection of isolated placements.

# INTERPRETATION RULES

1.  The uploaded chart is the source of truth.
2.  Verify placements before interpreting them.
3.  Never invent a degree, house placement, cusp, or aspect.
4.  Distinguish house cusp signs from signs contained inside houses.
5.  Account for interceptions when using quadrant house systems such as
    Placidus.
6.  Do not assume every chart has interceptions.
7.  Do not assume every chart has a stellium.
8.  Do not treat an empty house as unimportant.
9.  Interpret opposing houses together.
10. Treat astrology as a symbolic/interpretive framework rather than
    scientifically established causation.
11. If chart text is unreadable, state what cannot be determined rather
    than guessing.
12. When the chart itself prints a house table or planetary table,
    prioritize those exact values over visually estimating wheel
    positions.
13. Use exact degrees for factual extraction, but prioritize clear,
    accessible language in interpretation.
14. Distinguish structural observations ("five planets occupy the 10th")
    from interpretive conclusions ("this may emphasize public life").
15. Look for patterns that emerge across multiple placements rather than
    forcing every placement into one predetermined narrative.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# OUTPUT STYLE

The finished reading should be:

-   organized around the six axes
-   detailed but readable
-   specific to the supplied chart
-   explanatory rather than jargon-heavy
-   structurally precise
-   interpretive without being deterministic

Prefer:

**Placement → symbolism → axis tension → integration**

over generic descriptions of zodiac signs.

The reader should finish understanding not merely what their individual
placements mean, but **how the two halves of their chart continually
answer one another across the wheel.**
